The voices in your headphones

Most of the club's reading happens by audiobook. These are the narrators behind the series on the shelf.

  • Jeff Hays

    Dungeon Crawler Carl

    Voices both Carl and Princess Donut, with a full cast around him. Founder of Soundbooth Theater.

    Soundbooth Theater

  • Travis Baldree

    Cradle and Beware of Chicken

    Narrates both series.

    Narrator site

  • Andrea Parsneau

    The Wandering Inn

    Narrated the series through book 15. Erin Bennett takes over from book 16, King of Duels.

    Audible series

  • Johnathan McClain

    Tower of Jack

    Narrates the Tower of Jack series.

    Audible

  • Heath Miller

    Heretical Fishing

    Narrates every book in the series so far. It's on Hoopla, so check your library.

    Hoopla

  • Luke Daniels

    Iron Prince (Warformed: Stormweaver)

    Narrates Iron Prince and its sequel, Fire and Song.

    Publisher page

  • Tim Gerard Reynolds

    Red Rising

    Sole narrator of the original Red Rising trilogy.

    Audible

  • William Dufris

    Old Man's War

    Narrator of Old Man's War. He died in 2020.

    Wikipedia

  • Rob Inglis and Andy Serkis

    The Hobbit and The Lord of the Rings

    Two full recordings to choose from: the classic Rob Inglis reading and the newer Andy Serkis one.

    About the Serkis recording

More get added as the club listens.
