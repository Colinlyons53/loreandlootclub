Authors the club reads

The people who wrote the series the club keeps recommending. Vote on the home page for the one you want covered next.

  • Matt Dinniman

    Dungeon Crawler Carl

    Earth is flattened and turned into a televised alien dungeon. Carl and his ex-girlfriend's cat, Princess Donut, have to survive it floor by floor.

    Author site

  • Sean Loomer

    Tower of Jack

    A LitRPG series published by Mountaindale Press, four books so far.

    Audible

  • Jack_Golightly

    The Gift of Loot

    The System arrives on Earth and Thomas gets a Gift that makes every monster he kills drop loot. A LitRPG apocalypse that isn't grimdark, serialized on Royal Road.

    Royal Road

  • pirateaba

    The Wandering Inn

    A long-running web serial about Erin Solstice, who lands in a fantasy world and ends up running an inn. New chapters are published free online.

    Read the serial

  • Will Wight

    Cradle

    A twelve-book progression fantasy that follows Lindon from the weakest member of his clan upward, one advancement at a time.

    Author site

  • Casualfarmer

    Beware of Chicken

    A cultivation story where the hero quits the sect to run a farm, and his rooster starts cultivating instead. It began as a serial on Royal Road.

    Audible

  • Haylock Jobson

    Heretical Fishing

    A cozy fantasy about a man who only wants to fish, in a world that keeps getting in the way. It began as a serial on Royal Road.

    Publisher page

  • Bryce O'Connor and Luke Chmilenko

    Iron Prince (Warformed: Stormweaver)

    Military academy sci-fi with a progression system. Book two, Fire and Song, is by Bryce O'Connor.

    Publisher page

  • Pierce Brown

    Red Rising

    Darrow, a miner on Mars, infiltrates the ruling class to bring it down. Not the club's top pick, but a great entry point into sci-fi books.

    Author site

  • John Scalzi

    Old Man's War

    John Perry enlists in an interstellar army on his 75th birthday. Military sci-fi with a sense of humor.

    Author site

  • J.R.R. Tolkien

    The Hobbit and The Lord of the Rings

    Middle-earth. Where most of the fantasy shelf started.

    Tolkien Estate

More get added as the club reads them.
